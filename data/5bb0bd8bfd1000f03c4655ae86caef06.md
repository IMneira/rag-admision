**Tipo de evento**  
Academic

__

**Área que organiza**  
International Business

__

**Temas**  
English Test

__

**Inicio**  
12 July, 10:30 hrs.

__

**Término**  
12 july, 12:00 hrs.

__

**Lugar**  
Online o presencial

**ENGLISH LANGUAGE LEVEL REQUIREMENT!!**

To qualify for a place in the program, candidates must demonstrate a B2 (CEFR) equivalent or better English Language proficiency. Applicants must pass the English Language Test offered by the UANDES School of Business and Economics (taken on Campus or online) or submit the results of an international English test certifying the required proficiency (CAE, FCE, IGCSE, AS & Levels, IELTS, IB English A, IB English B, PTE Academic, PEIC, OTE, TOEFL IBT).
