### UANDES School of Business and Economics

# International Business  


##### 

##### Why International Business at Universidad de los Andes?

The International Business program of Universidad de los Andes allows students to gain an international perspective. By sharing learning experiences in class sessions with students from diverse cultural and national backgrounds, and spending a mandatory semester studying abroad in one of our partner universities, students acquire the knowledge and develop the necessary skills to thrive in a global environment.

**Top 3 undergraduate business school in Chile**

**Top 2 in employability**

**Over 2,500 graduates**

**76% of faculty holds a Ph.D.**

#### Career Prospects

The International Business program of Universidad de los Andes allows students to gain an international business perspective. During their studies, students share learning experiences in class sessions with students from diverse cultural and national backgrounds, and spend a mandatory semester studying abroad in one of our partner universities. After completing the 8-semester curriculum, students acquire the skills to work in global workplaces and the knowledge regarding different cultures and business practices, both necessary to thrive in a global environment. Graduates can pursue careers in areas such as International Business, Finance, Marketing, Human Resources Management and Global Strategy, in Chile or other countries of the world.

[ Course Structure __](https://admision.uandes.cl/docs/default-source/carreras/documentos-area-ingenieria-y-administracion/international-business/malla-international-business-2025.pdf?sfvrsn=79eb8e2d_5)

##### Degree

Bachelor of Science in International Business (Licenciado en Negocios Internacionales)

##### Program length

8 semesters

##### Leading to

Ingeniería Comercial (+ 2 semesters)

## PROGRAM FEATURES  


**4-year program** : pioneer undergraduate degree program in International Business in Chile.  


  
  


**International semester** : study abroad one or two semesters during the 4th year at one of our partner universities, including Bristol (UK), Northeastern (USA), WU Vienna (Austria) and Macquarie (Australia).********

  
  


**Bilingual** : learn Finance, Marketing, Economics, Strategy and International Business in English.****

  
  


******Internationally recognized** : the BSc in International Business is a well-known degree and recognized around the world. Graduates may continue studying an MBA or other master’s degree in Chile or abroad.

  
  


**Multicultural environment** : share learning experiences with students from other countries.****

****  
  


**Ingeniería Comercial** : after your 4-year Bachelor of Science in International Business, you can voluntarily study two additional semesters, obtaining the Ingeniería Comercial professional degree.****

## ENGLISH LANGUAGE LEVEL REQUIREMENT

To qualify for a place in the program, candidates must demonstrate a B2 (CEFR) equivalent or better English Language proficiency. 

English Test

International English Certificate

### UANDES English Test

Applicants must pass the English Language Test offered by the UANDES School of Business and Economics (taken on Campus or online)   
All students from out of Santiago willing to take the English Test only are entitled to do so. We'll send you an email with the details to access the testing platform one week before the test date. If you nevertheless prefer to take the test online on campus, please let us know responding the confirmation email you receive when signing up for the test.   
The next available date is July 12th.   
[ Sign up to take the test ](https://webapp.uandes.cl/form/form10)

### International English Certificate

Applicants can submit the results of an international English test certifying the required proficiency (CAE, FCE, IGCSE, AS & Levels, IELTS, IB English A, IB English B, PTE Academic, PEIC, OTE, TOEFL IBT).   
[ Applicants may submit their certificates here ](https://webapp.uandes.cl/form/form10)
